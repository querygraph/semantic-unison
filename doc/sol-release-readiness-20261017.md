## Evidence binding review

`tests/qualification/sol-acceptance-pin-review-20261017.json` reads selected receipt
JSON and compares only explicitly keyed `src/...` SHA pairs (including nested
participants). It records unique `(path, recorded SHA)` pairs, which may include
several historical versions of one file. A receipt with zero extracted pairs has
not been rebound: its pins may be indirect or have a different schema. Changed
pins do not erase historical evidence, but they prohibit claiming a fresh current
execution. For example, old merge receipts contain62 changed source bindings and
the older scheduled-worker receipt22; current full builds alone do not rerun their
fault, transfer or scheduling controls.

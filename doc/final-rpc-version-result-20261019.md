Source inventory confirms the removal is an ancestor of8772, its supported version list is `[1]`, its v0 converters are absent and old non-upgraded headers are refused. **The SOL ordinary-transfer fallback requirement for RPCv0 is not achieved by this result.** Successful current/upstream and installed2.53.5 transfers negotiate supported RPC1; they do not prove a v0 transfer. Reintroducing v0 would entail deliberately restoring upstream-removed compiler-sensitive marshal conversions and reviewing all affected wire payloads, or explicitly revising the supported-peer product requirement. Neither has happened. This remains a visible requirement mismatch rather than an unexecutable test being silently counted as passed. No production transport behavior changed here.
